Publications

SVGEval: A Vision-Grounded Framework for Perceptual-Quality Benchmarking and Evaluation in Text-to-SVG Generation

Yiming Wang*, Ye Chen*, Hanqi Chen, and Bingbing Ni. ECCV 2026, accepted / to appear.

A vision-grounded benchmark for SVG generation quality assessment, covering text-to-SVG evaluation, human annotation, and interpretable SVG scoring models.

ECCV 2026 arXiv

LA4VLA: Learning to Act without Seeing via Language-Action Pretraining

Tao Lin, Yuxin Du, Yiran Mao, Zewei Ye, Yilei Zhong, Bing Cheng, Yiming Wang, et al. arXiv, 2026.

Language-action pretraining for VLA models, studying how language-action supervision transfers to robot perception and control.
